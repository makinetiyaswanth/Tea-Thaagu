Our Heritage & Craft

Reimagining the Indian Adda

Born from a love for slow-brewed spices and specialty estate coffee, Kesar & Kadak is a modern sanctuary for conversation, art, and honest food.

Est. 2024

Rooted in Tradition, Brewed for Today.

For generations, Indian tea stalls and coffee houses have been the beating heart of neighborhoods—places where poets, students, and neighbors gathered over steaming kulhads.

At Kesar & Kadak, we partner directly with small-holder spice growers in Kerala, saffron harvesters in Pampore, and shade-grown coffee estates in Chikmagalur to bring transparency and modern culinary technique to every cup and plate.

Our Philosophy

What Defines Our Kitchen

01. Direct-Origin Spices

We stone-grind our green cardamom, black pepper, and cinnamon in small batches every morning to preserve volatile essential oils.

02. Zero Refined Sugar

Our house chais and bakery glazes are sweetened exclusively with organic palm jaggery, raw forest honey, and date syrup.

03. Artisanal Clay & Brass

Every beverage is served in handcrafted terracotta kulhads or hammered brass davaras crafted by local artisan clusters.
